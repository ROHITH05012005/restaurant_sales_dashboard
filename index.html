About This Dataset

This dashboard displays analytics compiled from a completely synthetic sales dataset comprising 10,950 transaction records generated programmatically. It models a fictional fast-food burger chain located in premium areas across Bengaluru, India.

Simulated Business Rules

  • Monsoon Impacts: Rainy days systematically reduce sales and walk-in footfall by 10-20%.
  • Region Profiles: Electronic City shows high weekday sales (IT parks), while MG Road exhibits strong weekend peaks.
  • Peak Hours: Whitefield displays lunch-heavy peaks (60-65% share) while Koramangala is dinner-heavy (65-70% share).
  • Math Consistency: 100% exact alignment on GST (5%), discount rates (5%/10%), gross sales, and average bill size calculations.
